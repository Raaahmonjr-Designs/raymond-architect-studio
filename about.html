[01] Practice Philosophy

Bridging structural precision, spatial artistry, and physical permanent installations.

Studio Raymond operates as a multidisciplinary architectural and visual design practice. We explore the space where physical structures, 3D digital visualization, and painted murals intersect to define human experiences within physical spaces.

Founded by Raymond, the practice emphasizes minimalist architectural aesthetics, raw materiality, and spatial clarity. From initial conceptual 3D drafting to high-impact physical murals and structural executions, every intervention is crafted with meticulous attention to silence, scale, and context.
